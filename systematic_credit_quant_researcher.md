# Quantitative Researcher — Systematic Credit

**Level:** Experienced

**Location:** [New York]

**Department:** Trading, Research and Machine Learning

We are building a systematic credit business that trades corporate bonds, credit derivatives and fixed income ETFs. We are looking for a quantitative researcher who can turn noisy credit market data into predictive signals, better prices and stronger trading decisions.

You will work directly with portfolio managers, traders and engineers. You will build the data, signals, risk and cost models that the desk relies on, test them against realistic trading conditions and bring them into production. This is a role for someone who wants to own the full research problem: from finding a new dataset or idea to understanding how a signal performs once it is live.

## What you'll do

- Research and develop predictive signals for single-name corporate bonds, CDS and credit ETFs. Use statistical, structural and fundamental models, time-series methods and machine learning to model how credit spreads move.
- Build risk factor and transaction cost models for credit. Account for bid–ask spreads, dealer and electronic liquidity, issue size, market impact and the cost of exiting a position.
- Build and own a research-grade credit data pipeline. Ingest, clean and normalise TRACE, dealer runs, electronic trading venue feeds and alternative data, and keep it point-in-time so that research reflects what was known at the time.
- Design and maintain a shared backtesting platform that lets researchers and traders test signal ideas quickly against a standardised, bias-free simulation. Use automated parameter tuning where it helps, without letting it overfit.
- Connect signals to trading. Feed alphas into pricing, quoting and portfolio construction across bonds, CDS indices and credit ETFs, and use numerical optimisation where the problem calls for it.
- Run P&L attribution across signals, risk factors and costs. Find where models work, where they decay and where they need to improve.
- Search for and prioritise new ideas and datasets with real alpha potential, and drop the ones that do not survive realistic testing.
- Partner with developers to build modular, well-tested, high-performance analytics libraries and simulation tools, and help shape the desk's next research capabilities.

## Who you are

- You have at least two to three years of experience developing statistical or fundamental alpha signals and risk factors in single-name credit, equities or options. Direct research experience in corporate bonds or credit derivatives is particularly valuable.
- You understand bond pricing, credit spreads and the main credit instruments, and you know why credit data is harder to work with than equity data: sparse trades, stale quotes, dealer-driven liquidity and reporting delays.
- You are strong in probability, statistics, linear algebra and time-series analysis, and you know how to apply machine learning to financial data without fooling yourself.
- You write production-quality Python (pandas, NumPy, SciPy, scikit-learn) with sound object-oriented design, testing and CI/CD practices. You are comfortable with SQL and high-performance time-series databases.
- You have a postgraduate degree in mathematics, statistics, physics, economics, finance, computer science, operations research or a related quantitative field, preferably a PhD.
- You are intellectually honest about results. You test your assumptions, report what fails as clearly as what works and change course when the data disagrees.
- You communicate clearly and work well both on your own and with portfolio managers, traders and engineers.

Experience with FICC, credit or option pricing models, numerical optimisation, KDB+/q, large tick-level datasets or building self-service research and backtesting platforms would be useful. It is not required for this role today.

## What you'll get

The opportunity to help build a systematic credit business from an early stage and see your research tested in live markets. You will work closely with people across portfolio management, trading, research and engineering, with direct ownership of signals, models and infrastructure that drive the desk's performance.
