# Credit Trader — Principal Risk and Credit Derivatives Market Making

**Level:** Experienced

**Location:** [New York]

**Department:** Trading, Research and Machine Learning

We are building a credit trading business that combines market making in credit derivatives with principal risk-taking in corporate bonds. We are looking for a trader who can make markets in CDS indices, single-name CDS and index swaptions, and who can also run positional and relative-value risk on the firm's own capital.

You will be measured on the P&L you generate. You will work directly with credit analysts, quantitative researchers and engineers, using fundamentals, market structure and data to decide what to own, what to hedge and what to price. This is a role for someone who wants to own the full trading problem: from forming a view to sizing it, executing it and managing it when markets move.

## What you'll do

- Make markets in index CDS (CDX IG and HY), single-name CDS and European-style index swaptions. Negotiate and execute trades, and manage the inventory and risk that the flow creates.
- Take and manage principal risk in investment-grade, high-yield and stressed corporate bonds and CDS within agreed limits. Express directional and sector views on spreads, credit cycles and leverage.
- Trade relative value across cash bonds and CDS (the basis), issuer curves, capital structures, indices and their constituents, and credit ETFs. Understand what each trade costs to fund, hedge and exit.
- Manage risk across the book using spread DV01 (the dollar change for a one basis point move in spreads), interest-rate duration, jump-to-default, correlation and option Greeks. Run scenario analysis for spread shocks, defaults and volatility moves.
- Help automate the electronic side of the business. Work with quants and engineers on pricing models, automated responses to requests for quote (RFQs), and hit-rate and quoting logic for electronic credit venues.
- Find and test trade ideas using backtesting and regression analysis. Identify dislocations, and separate real opportunities from stale prices or thin liquidity.
- Analyse macroeconomic and company-specific drivers of credit. Partner with credit analysts on issuer health and produce clear, timely views on events that move credit markets.
- Build analytical tools that screen markets, monitor positioning and manage desk risk. Review fills, quotes and P&L to find where the desk makes and loses money.

## Who you are

- You have at least two years of experience trading credit at a bank, dealer, hedge fund or proprietary trading firm. Hands-on market making in index CDS is particularly valuable.
- You have managed principal risk in corporate bonds or CDS and can explain how you sized, hedged and exited positions, including the ones that lost money.
- You understand the economics of credit trades: carry, roll-down, funding, the CDS–bond basis, index skew, default and recovery risk, and how liquidity disappears in stress.
- You are comfortable with option risk on credit, including index swaption Greeks, volatility and scenario analysis.
- You know how electronic credit markets work, including MarketAxess, Tradeweb, interdealer brokers, bilateral dealer relationships and TRACE data.
- You are strong in statistics and data analysis, and you use Python or similar tools to build screens, backtests and risk tools. You can work with engineers on production trading systems.
- You have at least a bachelor's degree in economics, econometrics, finance, mathematics or a related field. A quantitative postgraduate degree is a plus.
- You make decisions with incomplete information, cut positions when the thesis breaks and communicate clearly with traders, analysts, researchers and engineers.

Experience with distressed debt, capital-structure arbitrage, credit index tranches or systematic credit strategies would be useful as the business expands. It is not required for this role today.

## What you'll get

The opportunity to help build a credit trading business that combines market making with principal risk-taking, and to see your views tested in live markets. You will work closely with people across trading, research and engineering, with direct ownership of risk and decisions that affect the desk's performance.
