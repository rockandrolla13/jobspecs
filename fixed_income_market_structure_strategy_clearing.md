# Vice President — Fixed Income Market Structure, Strategy and Clearing

**Level:** Vice President

**Location:** [New York]

**Department:** Markets Strategy and Market Structure

Fixed income markets are changing faster than at any time since the financial crisis. US Treasuries are moving into central clearing. Trading in rates and credit keeps moving onto electronic platforms through all-to-all, portfolio trading and streaming. Capital rules, transparency regimes and new entrants keep shifting who provides liquidity and how. We are looking for someone who can see these changes early, explain what they mean and turn them into clear strategic decisions for the business.

This is not a trading or risk-taking role. You will be the business's expert on how fixed income markets work: who trades, where, how trades are cleared and settled, and where the economics are moving. You will work with senior management, product, sales, clearing, legal and regulatory teams and engineers. You will own the facts, the analysis and the recommendations that shape where we invest.

## What you'll do

- **Market intelligence.** Build and own the business's view of the fixed income market across Treasuries, repo, interest-rate swaps, futures and credit. Track volumes, market share, liquidity, trading protocols and client segments, using public and commercial data such as FINRA TRACE, DTCC, CFTC and SEC disclosures, venue and clearing house statistics, industry surveys and competitor filings.
- **Clearing strategy.** Analyse the move to central clearing for Treasury cash and repo, and its effect on dealers, the buy side, principal trading firms and venues. Compare clearing access models such as sponsored, agent and done-away clearing. Assess margin, default fund, netting, cross-margining and balance sheet economics across the main clearing houses for Treasuries, swaps and credit.
- **Market structure analysis.** Explain how electronification, all-to-all trading, portfolio trading, request-for-quote and streaming protocols, fixed income ETFs and non-bank liquidity providers are changing the market. Find where value is shifting between dealers, venues, clearing houses, data providers and clients.
- **Regulatory analysis.** Track rule changes that reshape market structure, such as clearing mandates, bank capital and leverage rules, trade transparency and reporting, and international equivalents. Turn each into a clear view of its business impact, and help draft responses to consultations.
- **Strategy and business cases.** Size markets, build business cases and pricing analyses for new products and services, and assess partnerships, investments and acquisitions. Write board and executive committee papers that lead with a recommendation and back it with evidence.
- **Industry and client voice.** Build relationships with clients, dealers, venues, clearing houses and industry bodies. Bring back what the market is planning before it shows up in the data.
- **Data and tools.** Build and maintain the datasets, models and dashboards behind the business's market intelligence, so that key questions can be answered quickly and repeatably.
- **Communication.** Produce regular market structure briefings for senior management, and explain complex topics simply to non-specialists inside and outside the firm.

## Who you are

- You have five to ten or more years of experience in fixed income market structure, strategy, product management, clearing or research. This may be at a bank, trading venue, clearing house, data provider, asset manager, consultancy or regulator.
- You understand how US Treasury, repo and interest-rate markets work in practice, from trade to clearing to settlement. Knowledge of credit, swaps or futures market structure is a strong plus.
- You understand clearing economics: margin models, default fund contributions, netting, cross-margining and how clearing affects bank balance sheets and capital.
- You follow the regulatory agenda closely and can explain how a rule change will alter behaviour, volumes and revenue across the market.
- You are strong with data. You can find, clean and analyse large public and commercial datasets using Python, SQL or similar tools, and you present findings clearly.
- You write well. You can turn a complex topic into a short paper with a clear recommendation, and you are comfortable presenting to senior management.
- You think in structured, commercial terms. You ask who pays, who benefits and what changes behaviour, and you change your view when the evidence changes.
- You have at least a bachelor's degree in economics, finance, mathematics or a related field. A postgraduate degree or professional qualification is a plus.

Experience with European or UK market structure, FX or equities clearing, consulting on capital markets strategy, or working at a regulator or industry body would be useful. It is not required for this role today.

## What you'll get

The opportunity to shape strategy at a time when fixed income market structure is being rebuilt. You will work closely with senior management and teams across the business, with direct ownership of the analysis and recommendations that decide where we compete and invest.
